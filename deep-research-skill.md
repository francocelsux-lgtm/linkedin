# Deep Research Skill

## Purpose

Gather signal before writing. This skill defines how to research topics, analyze the LinkedIn profile, scan competitors, and identify what content is gaining traction — so every post is grounded in data, not guesses.

---

## Research Layers

### Layer 1 — Profile Analysis (Own Account)

Understand the baseline before comparing to anyone else.

**What to analyze:**
- Top 10 posts by impressions and engagement rate
- Post formats that outperform (list, story, hot take, carousel, poll)
- Topics that drive comments vs. only likes
- Posting cadence: frequency and time-of-day patterns
- Follower growth correlated to specific posts
- Connection demographics (title, industry, seniority)

**Questions to answer:**
- What topics is our audience most interested in?
- Which hooks generated the highest click-through on "…see more"?
- What post length performs best (short burst vs. long form)?
- Are videos or images outperforming text-only posts?

---

### Layer 2 — Competitor Analysis

Map the landscape to find gaps and patterns.

**Who to track:**
- 3-5 direct competitors (same niche, similar audience size)
- 3-5 aspirational accounts (category leaders, 10x larger)
- 2-3 adjacent creators (different niche but same audience persona)

**What to record per competitor:**
```
Name:
Profile URL:
Follower count:
Avg. post frequency:
Top 3 performing post formats:
Recurring hooks/patterns:
Topics with highest engagement:
Topics they're NOT covering (gaps):
Comment quality (surface or deep discussion?):
```

**Gap analysis:**
- What questions does their audience ask in comments that aren't being answered?
- Which topics are oversaturated in the niche?
- Which angles are completely uncovered?

---

### Layer 3 — Topic Research

Go deep on the subject before writing a single word.

**Research protocol:**
1. Identify the core claim or insight of the post
2. Find 3+ credible sources (reports, studies, firsthand accounts)
3. Pull 1 specific data point or statistic to anchor the post
4. Find a counterargument — steelman it, then decide whether to address it
5. Identify the most surprising or counterintuitive element

**Research sources (priority order):**
1. Primary data: personal experiments, tests, internal metrics
2. Industry reports: published studies, annual surveys
3. Reputable journalists / analysts covering the space
4. Competitor posts (to see the angle that's already been taken)
5. Comments on popular posts (to find unanswered questions)

---

### Layer 4 — Viral Hook Research

Before writing the hook, study what stops scrolls in your niche.

**Hook pattern library — build and update continuously:**

| Pattern               | Example                                              |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Bold claim            | "Cold email is dead."                                |
| Surprising number     | "I sent 1,000 cold emails. Here's what happened."    |
| Mistake/failure       | "I lost $40k trusting this advice."                  |
| Contrarian take       | "Everyone says post daily. I disagree."              |
| Curiosity gap         | "The one LinkedIn mistake killing your reach."        |
| Personal story        | "3 years ago I quit my job with $0 saved."           |
| Social proof          | "How I grew 10k followers posting 2x/week."          |
| Direct challenge      | "You're writing LinkedIn posts wrong."               |
| List promise          | "7 things I wish I knew before building in public."  |
| Question              | "What if the advice everyone follows is backwards?"  |

**Research task:** Weekly, scan top 20 posts in niche. Extract the hook. Tag the pattern. Add to library.

---

### Layer 5 — Trend Detection

Catch topics while they're rising, not after they've peaked.

**Signals to monitor:**
- Trending hashtags in your niche on LinkedIn
- Topics with a spike in Google Trends (past 7 days)
- Viral threads on X (Twitter) that haven't hit LinkedIn yet
- New research papers or industry reports (published in last 30 days)
- New product launches or company announcements in your space
- Regulatory or market shifts that affect your audience

**Timing rule:** A trend post is 10x more valuable if you're 3 days early vs. 3 days late.

---

## Research Output Template

Before starting any post, fill this out:

```markdown
## Research Brief

**Post topic:** [one sentence]
**Core insight / claim:** [the single argument of this post]
**Proof / data point:** [specific stat, example, or story]
**Target audience segment:** [who specifically is this for?]
**Hook pattern selected:** [from hook library]
**Competitors who covered this:** [names + what angle they took]
**Our differentiated angle:** [what makes this different from what's out there]
**Potential objections:** [what will skeptics say?]
**Call to reflection / CTA:** [what do we want the reader to think or do?]
```
