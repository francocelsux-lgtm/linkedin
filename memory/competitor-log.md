# Competitor Log

Tracks observations about competitor LinkedIn accounts over time. Updated via `/research-trends [competitor name]` and during monthly deep-dives.

---

## How to Use

- When a competitor posts something that performs well, log it here
- Look for patterns across entries: what formats, hooks, and topics consistently win for them?
- Use gaps between competitors' coverage to find content opportunities for Celsux
