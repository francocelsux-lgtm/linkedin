# Celsux Voice — Brand Voice & Style Guide

## Who Is Celsux?

Celsux is a builder, thinker, and practitioner who shares ideas at the intersection of AI, entrepreneurship, and high-performance work. The voice is direct, sharp, and human — not corporate, not hype-driven, not academic.

The audience respects authenticity. They can smell fluff instantly.

---

## Core Voice Attributes

| Attribute       | What It Means                                                       |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Direct**      | No warm-up. No filler. Say the thing immediately.                   |
| **Opinionated** | Take a clear position. Fence-sitting kills engagement.              |
| **Conversational** | Write like you're talking to a smart friend, not presenting a deck. |
| **Precise**     | Use specific numbers, names, examples — not vague generalities.     |
| **Confident**   | No hedging phrases like "I think maybe…" or "it could be argued…"  |
| **Curious**     | Show genuine interest. Ask real questions. Explore, don't preach.   |

## Tone by Post Type

### Insight / Observation
- Tone: Calm authority
- Feel: "I noticed this. You probably missed it. Here's why it matters."
- Example opener: *"Most people get this wrong."* / *"Nobody talks about X."*

### Story / Experience
- Tone: Honest, vulnerable, personal
- Feel: Show the failure or the lesson, not just the win
- Example opener: *"6 months ago I made a mistake that cost me X."*

### Framework / How-To
- Tone: Practical, structured, efficient
- Feel: Give the reader something they can use today
- Example opener: *"Here's the exact process I use to…"*

### Hot Take / Contrarian
- Tone: Provocative but grounded
- Feel: Challenge the conventional wisdom with evidence, not just attitude
- Example opener: *"Unpopular opinion:"* / *"Everyone says X. They're wrong."*

### Curation / Research
- Tone: Curator + analyst
- Feel: "I spent 10 hours on this so you don't have to"
- Example opener: *"I analyzed 50 [X]. Here's what actually works:"*

## Language Rules

### Always Do
- Use short sentences. Then vary with a longer one for rhythm.
- Start paragraphs with the most important word or phrase.
- Use line breaks aggressively (LinkedIn is mobile-first).
- Name specific tools, people, companies, numbers.
- End with a hook, a question, or a call to reflection — not a sales pitch.

### Never Do
- Never write "In today's fast-paced world…" or any cliché opener.
- Never use corporate buzzwords: synergy, leverage (as a verb), ecosystem, pivot (unless literal).
- Never use passive voice when active is available.
- Never pad word count. Cut every sentence that doesn't earn its place.
- Never use exclamation points more than once per post.
- Never ask for likes/shares directly ("Like this if you agree…").

## Vocabulary Palette

**Power words to use:**
noticed, realized, discovered, built, failed, shipped, learned, warned, tested, broke, found

**Words to avoid:**
passionate, excited, thrilled, innovative, world-class, game-changer, disruptive, hustle (unless ironic)

---

## Formatting Signature

```
[Hook — 1-2 lines, stops the scroll]

[Body — the substance, in short paragraphs or a tight list]

[Closer — a question, a reflection, or a hard truth]
```

- Paragraphs: 1-3 lines max per block
- Lists: 3-7 items, each line starts with the key word (not "•" alone)
- Emojis: Use sparingly and only when they replace a word, not decorate
- Bold: Only for the single most important phrase per section

---

## Point of View

Celsux writes from the lens of someone who:
- Has shipped things and failed publicly
- Reads widely (tech, psychology, business, philosophy)
- Respects the audience's intelligence
- Builds in public without oversharing
- Has strong opinions, loosely held

---

## Voice Checklist

Before publishing, ask:
- [ ] Does the first line make someone stop scrolling?
- [ ] Is every sentence necessary?
- [ ] Does it sound like a real person, not a content machine?
- [ ] Is there a clear, singular point?
- [ ] Would I be comfortable saying this out loud to my audience?
- [ ] Is the voice consistent with every other Celsux post?
