## Voice & Positioning Gaps

### Strengths to Amplify
- **Deep credibility:** 23 years + 3,000 events is a moat. Most competitors can't say that. Use it more often, not as a number in a headline but as proof in stories.
- **LATAM expertise:** Deep regional knowledge and network is a genuine differentiator vs. international agencies.
- **Practitioner, not theorist:** Luis has run the events himself. That lived experience should appear in content constantly.
- **Club Med origin:** This is a powerful story element — it signals that the philosophy behind Celsux came from experiencing the world's best at curated human experiences.

### Gaps to Close
- **Thought leadership visibility:** The profile is well-positioned but content output likely doesn't match the depth of 23 years of insight. Publishing consistently would compound authority fast.
- **Outcome-first language:** Celsux's public positioning (website, older social) leans toward features ("we do team building, events, outdoor training"). Shifting to outcomes ("teams that perform differently after our events") is the differentiator.
- **The "before and after" story:** The most compelling content for RRHH buyers is: "Company X had this cultural problem → they ran this experience → here's what changed." These stories are not being told at scale yet.
- **Data and insight posts:** There's an untapped opportunity to share original data or observations: "After 3,000 events, here are the 3 things that determine whether a team building actually works."

## LinkedIn Content Opportunities

### High-potential content formats for this profile:

**1. "After 3,000 events, I've noticed..." (Insight posts)**
Premise: Pattern recognition from thousands of events that no competitor has access to.
Example hook: *"After running 3,000+ corporate events, I can tell in the first 5 minutes whether a team building will actually transform the culture — or just waste everyone's time."*

**2. The RRHH Reframe (Hot takes for HR professionals)**
Premise: Challenge the conventional view of what HR's job is.
Example hook: *"La mayoría de los equipos de RRHH están organizando eventos. Los mejores están diseñando experiencias. La diferencia es enorme."*

**3. Before/After Culture Stories (Proof posts)**
Premise: Anonymized case studies of companies that ran experiences and what changed.
Example hook: *"Una empresa de 500 personas tenía un problema de cultura que 3 años de talleres no habían resuelto. Lo resolvimos en 2 días. Así fue."*

**4. The Event Behind the Event (Behind-the-scenes)**
Premise: Show what goes into designing a corporate experience. Humanizes the brand.
Example hook: *"Lo que los asistentes ven: una actividad de equipo de 2 horas. Lo que nosotros vemos: 6 semanas de diseño estratégico. Esto es lo que pasa detrás."*

**5. Contrarian takes on team building**
Premise: Call out what doesn't work — builds trust with skeptical RRHH buyers.
Example hook: *"El 80% de los team buildings no cambian nada. Y las empresas siguen pagando por ellos. Aquí está el problema."*
